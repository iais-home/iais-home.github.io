Faculty & Leadership

Jee-Hang Lee, Ph.D.

President & Senior Fellow (Professor)

Dr. Jee-Hang Lee leads the Institute for Advanced Intelligence Study (IAIS). With a Ph.D. from the University of Bath (2015) and extensive independent research leadership, his work spans Computational Neuroscience, Digital Phenomics, and Reinforcement Learning Meta-Control. Under his direction, IAIS bridges pure theoretical modeling with robust algorithmic implementations, establishing a novel dual-track research paradigm. His work is widely published in premier venues including Cell Reports, ACM CHI, JMIR, Scientific Reports, and RLDM.
