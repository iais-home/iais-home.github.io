About the Institute

The Institute for Advanced Intelligence Study (IAIS) is a premier independent research institution dedicated to probing the depths of human and machine cognition.

Our Dual-Track Philosophy

Inspired by the pure academic governance of the Santa Fe Institute (SFI) and the decentralized, engineer-driven execution of Igalia, IAIS operates on a unique Dual-Track Structure.

We bridge the gap between theoretical rigor and system implementation. Our Academic Track (Professors and Fellows) leads pure scientific discovery in computational neuroscience, logic, and cognitive control. Simultaneously, our Engineering Track (Chief and Staff Research Engineers) translates these formal models into robust, real-world AI architectures and digital therapeutic (DTx) software.

From Neurons to Norms

IAIS is entirely focused on grand challenges: from decoding biological neural dynamics (Brain) to predicting human behavior via digital phenomics (Phenotype), and establishing formal methods for safe AI (Norms and Law). Our agile, independent structure allows us to execute "In the wild" longitudinal cohort deployments and continuous model optimization that traditional academic environments struggle to support.
