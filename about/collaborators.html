Mission & History

The Institute for Advanced Intelligence Study (IAIS) was founded to probe the deepest principles of natural and artificial intelligence, operating as an autonomous intellectual sanctuary free from short-term commercial demands.

Our Mission

IAIS bridges pure computational neuroscience with rigorous formal verification. Our core objective is to uncover how mammalian neural circuits execute meta-control and to synthesize these insights into verifiable, trustworthy artificial general intelligence.

The Dual-Track Model

Combining the pure academic ethos of the Santa Fe Institute (SFI) with the engineer-owned autonomy of Igalia, IAIS maintains two symbiotic tracks:

  • Academic Track (Faculty & Fellows): Dedicated to mathematical modeling, biological hypothesis formulation, and publishing world-leading scholarship in venues such as Cell Reports, JMIR, and ACM CHI.
  • Engineering Track (Research Engineers): Dedicated to architecting resilient software infrastructure, low-latency biosignal processing pipelines, and in-the-wild digital therapeutic benchmarks.
