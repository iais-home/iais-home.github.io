Organisation

IAIS is organised for research. A lean administrative structure supports four research centres, and an independent advisory board guides their scientific direction.

  • President: The President leads the institute and holds final responsibility for its research and operations.
  • Advisory Board: The Advisory Board stands between the President and the research centres. Its members review the scientific agenda and advise on the direction of each centre.
  • Operational Office: The Operational Office manages finance, contracts and partnerships. The Human Resource Office, which sits within it, oversees recruitment and the development of our people.
  • Research Office: The Research Office coordinates the four research centres, manages projects and secures research funding.

The Dual-Track Model

IAIS combines the academic ethos of the Santa Fe Institute with the researcher-owned autonomy of Igalia. Our people work on two tracks. Each track has its own expertise, and every project needs both.

Faculty & Fellows

Academic Track

The Academic Track defines the science. Faculty and fellows formulate hypotheses, build mathematical and computational models, and publish in leading venues such as Cell Reports, JMIR and ACM CHI.

Research Engineers

Engineering Track

The Engineering Track builds the systems. Research engineers design resilient software infrastructure, low-latency biosignal pipelines and in-the-wild benchmarks for digital therapeutics.
