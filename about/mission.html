Mission & History

The Institute for Advanced Intelligence Study (IAIS) explores natural intelligence through computational approaches and integrates what it learns into artificial intelligence. Our guiding principle is simple:

Probe Intelligence to Advance Intelligence.

Our Mission

We focus on the fundamental computational mechanisms that underlie intelligence, rather than on building ever-larger architectures. We believe that when cognitive processes are understood well enough to be expressed as general algorithms, those algorithms become artificial intelligence. Our work therefore begins with the mind and the brain, and it ends with principles that machines can share.

What We Study

We address fundamental questions about the human brain, mind, behaviour and society, and we seek to connect them with the domains that shape human life most directly: law, medicine and pharmacology. We approach these questions through computational and formal methods, analytical approaches, and human-centred approaches. Our current research focuses on four themes:

  • Computational modelling of the human brain and mind
  • Computational analysis of legal and normative decision-making
  • Psychology of AI and digital physiology
  • AI and human society

How We Work

IAIS brings together three institutional models, each of which governs a different aspect of the institute.

  • Academic model (Santa Fe Institute): We pursue fundamental problems for their own sake and across disciplinary boundaries. IAIS operates on a fully project-based system: researchers who join us define a problem, write a proposal, conduct the research and share the results openly.
  • Governance model (Igalia): IAIS is a researcher-owned institute. Although it was incorporated as a for-profit company, all equity belongs to the researchers who work here, and we accept no external investment. This structure allows us to pursue academic goals alone.
  • Operational model (Fraunhofer): We dedicate our capacity to problems that are fundamental, foundational and capable of transforming society, and we turn that research into knowledge that partners can use.

Why It Matters

This approach is one of the first of its kind in Korea, and we regard it as a meaningful step forward. Government-driven research models are often shaped by shifting policies and trends, whereas IAIS is built to pursue fundamental questions and to carry their answers through to real-world problems.

We are ready to collaborate with companies of every kind and to transfer our findings to them. We also welcome research that is driven by public interest or simply by curiosity. Anyone who brings a problem they wish to solve, together with the funding to support it, will find us ready to work alongside them.

Our History

IAIS was founded on 3 December 2024 by a group of researchers who came from cognitive science, AI, law, psychology and medicine. Since then, our work has appeared in highly ranked JCR journals and has been presented at flagship conferences in decision-making and neuroscience, as well as at top-tier conferences in computer science.

Looking Ahead

We will continue to pursue creative questions and in-depth research, and we aim to build foundational work together with leading universities and institutes worldwide and with researchers across Korea. Our work has no disciplinary boundaries. We will keep conducting research of the highest standard, securing the funding that sustains it, and quietly extending the frontiers of knowledge.
